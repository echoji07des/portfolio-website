We may collect the following data:

  • First and last name;

  • Email address;
  • Other data that the user voluntarily provides through feedback, registration, or service order forms.

Technical data may also be collected automatically:

  • IP address;
  • Browser and device type;
  • Data about visits to Website pages (analytics and interaction logs).

Purpose of Collecting Personal Data

Personal data is used for the following purposes:

  • Providing design and informational services (Legal basis: performance of a contract);
  • Communicating with the user, including consultations and responding to inquiries (Legal basis: legitimate interest or contract performance);
  • Processing and fulfilling orders (Legal basis: performance of a contract);
  • Sending informational and marketing materials (Legal basis: explicit user consent);
  • Improving the operation of the Website and the quality of services (Legal basis: legitimate interest);
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements (Legal basis: legal obligation).

Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.

Protection of Personal Data

The Website Administration takes all necessary technical and organizational measures to protect personal data from unauthorized access, loss, destruction, or disclosure.

Only authorized persons have access to personal data.

Transfer of Personal Data to Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. Users’ personal data is not transferred to third parties, except in the following cases:

  • With the user’s explicit consent;
  • When necessary to provide services, such as payment processors, hosting providers, analytics tools, and email marketing services;
  • In cases provided for by applicable law.

As we operate internationally, your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside of your country of residence (including Ukraine, the EU, or the US). When transferring data from the EEA, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) to protect your information.

Use of Cookies

The Website uses cookies for:

  • Proper operation of the Website (essential cookies);
  • Analytics and improving the user experience;
  • Marketing purposes.

For users in the EEA, non-essential cookies (such as analytics and marketing cookies) are used only after obtaining your explicit consent via our cookie banner. You can also change cookie settings in your browser or refuse to use them at any time.

User Rights (Including GDPR & US Privacy Rights)

Depending on your location, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: Request information about and a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • Right to Rectification: Demand the modification or correction of inaccurate personal data;
  • Right to Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): Request the deletion of your personal data under certain conditions;
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: Object to the processing of your data for direct marketing or based on legitimate interests;
  • Right to Data Portability: Request the transfer of your data to another organization or directly to you;
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent to the processing of personal data at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal;
  • Right to Non-Discrimination (US/California): We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact the Website Administration using the contact details below. If you are located in the EEA, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

The compensation is included in the estimate or agreement and is paid together with the prepayment or the first stage, unless agreed otherwise.

If the Client requests the restriction after the work has started or been completed, the Designer may accept or decline the request. If the Designer agrees, the compensation is also paid, in an amount agreed separately.
